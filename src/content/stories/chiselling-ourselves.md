But other one , it had not moved at all from its tree from the very day that he had started training them. After all the frustration due to attempt in making falcon to fly, prince decided to take help from someone who is expert in understanding a nature or birds and ordered his servants to find such a person. One fine day, Prince got surprised by seeing that falcons started flying above the palace. He asked his servants to get the doer of this miracle. Surprisingly, they brought a simple farmer. Prince asked the farmer, how did he achieve that, farmer replied “I Simply Cut the Branch of Tree”.

We are all made to fly and to live with unimaginable talent, but we remaining sit in our comfort zone, not willing to venture out because of fear. > ***Do not Hurry or Do not Worry !!!***

There lived a farmer , who planted a spinach and mango seeds and cared for them well. Farmer was delighted to see spinach quickly grows out from the earth with bright green colour after a few days. But he highly disappointed as nothing come out from the mango seed. But he never gave up. After a year’s spinach sprouts out vibrant, luxuriant , so greenish and the mango seed nothing came out . One more year crossed, still he did not quit. One more year passed , farmer saw a tiny a sprout emerges from the earth, and it is so tiny compared to spinach, but in a next six-month mango tree shot up to its height. What do you Observe here? In those 3 to 4 years mango growing its root and gave it all what it needs to survive in upcoming years. Isn’t it true LIFE does not give you a challenge which you cannot handle? Take a difficulty which you are facing now, know for sure that you have a capacity to handle it.

Remember all our time during struggles , you been developing like a mango tree. Both Spinach and Mango are different , but it makes a life beautiful, healthier by serving a different purpose.

Wait for your TIME , you will reach your destiny , Your Destiny, not someone is else. Remember that India is almost 10 hours ahead of USA, but that does not make India faster and USA slower. So, relax wait for your TIME, you neither early nor too late, just on TIME.

> ***Journey of Life is important.***

No right things are always right, and no wrong things are always wrong. I remember some years back , I read a story of 5 friends who experienced beautiful moments of their life. On one of their trekking , they got lost in vast forest and they were trying to find the way out. First person said that he going to follow his intuition and he decided to go left from where they are standing. The second man said that he decided to go right. Third man said that he decided to go back in the same way that they came across and he believed that is the safest way. Fourth person said that he decides to walk forward as he believed that he is on right track. But fifth man said, I will climb up in tree to find the right path or shortest path. And they all started their journey in the path decided .

We may think Fifth man is right . Since he rises above the situation and see a larger picture. This decision sounds good right to most of us. Yes of course but there is a twist in story. Let understand them. Finding the own path gaining you a different experience. It just not about right or wrong. First man reaches a town after a while. A man who went right got a situation to fight with wild ones , but now he is experienced in forest trek. The man who went back joins with the next group of trekkers and what happened, he got a friend right ? Man, who went straight found a farm and had a nice time and man on a tree found a shortest path.

Journey exposed each one of them in own unique ways. Life is an exam , if might wait if you copy others, you cannot live with others experience. Your current chapter is not same as someone’s current chapter.

**You can learn from others , but not live with others experience.**

> ***What’s your value.***

Do you think you are not recognized for your work? Do you feel bored ? Have you felt you are not a valuable ? If so , what is your value? How do you define it? Will see ….

This is a real incident happened to me. I have got a valuable old camera while cleaning my home. I believe it have a certain value and, I want to check that value. So, I thought to check the resale value of the camera. First, I took the camera to a camera resale and repair shop to check the value of it. After checking my camera, shopkeeper said that he can give me a $100 for this. Then I left from there and moved to a pawn shop to check collateral value of my camera. After seeing my camera, pawn shop owner said, can’t give anything for this scrap. it is not going to be useful for us. Then I went to antique museum where all old things are showcased. I showed that to a manager of the museum , he exclaimed that “ this is priceless , what a quality of leather cover and German made metals” I can give you a million dollars for this. I was really gobsmacked with his words. But I did not sell it still. Do you know why? I do not want to stab the duck before it lays golden egg.

Now I understand , you will be valued if you are in right place. In camera reselling and repair shop, reseller can see only nuts and bolts on that. In pawn shop, for collaterals they will look everything based on market value. But museum where old things are showcased see beyond the material, the emotions, the war behind the camera, people who used the camera, right? If you felt that you are not valued , do not get frustrated , do not felt bored. Seek help from those who see your real value , they may be your senior persons in your office.

There is a no letter that cannot be turned into a book, there is no plant or tree cannot have a medical value, here is no person in the world who does not value. Please remember this in your life.
