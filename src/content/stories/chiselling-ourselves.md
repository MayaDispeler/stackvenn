It’s never going to be a MYSTERY anymore…

Every time I think about motivation, I end up in a situation that exposes my own problem rather than someone else’s. So here I am, pushing self-help content, showing people how to live more successfully. Then I start my own investigation and realize I have many showstoppers of my own. This is one of those articles. It is all about seeing **yourself**, as in the image above. Too often, it is overlooked or mistaken for something else. Its impact is tremendous and ongoing. Learn to avoid excessive regrets, and you will unchain an innovation that was always there, waiting for you.

Keep in mind, this article is completely about ourselves, or maybe yourself. It is not meant as motivation, because I have never believed such a word exists.

> ***Set our Mind, Ready to Take off like a Falcon.***

There was a prince who received a special gift from his father: two radiant, beautiful falcons. The prince decided to train those falcons and handed them over to a man who was an expert in training falcons. After a couple of years, the falcon trainer told the prince that one of the falcons was doing really well. But the other one had not moved from its tree since the very day he started training them. After all the frustration of trying to make the falcon fly, the prince decided to take help from someone who was an expert in understanding nature and birds, and ordered his servants to find such a person. One fine day, the prince was surprised to see the falcons flying above the palace. He asked his servants to bring him the doer of this miracle. Surprisingly, they brought a simple farmer. The prince asked the farmer how he had achieved it, and the farmer replied, “I Simply Cut the Branch of the Tree”.

We are all made to fly and to live with unimaginable talent, but we remain sitting in our comfort zone, unwilling to venture out because of fear. But the truth is, the possibilities are countless and remain unidentified. So, most of our lives end up mediocre instead of exciting. Do you agree fear is stopping you? Then what are you waiting for? **Release the Anchor and Get Ready to Move**

> ***Will you be able to avoid mistakes always?***

How many of us have made a mistake in academics, at the office, in the family or in any other place? Have we ever regretted it after the mistake? But have you noticed that life allows us to correct those mistakes, like an eraser attached to a pencil? Of course, some mistakes cannot be corrected, and their impact is bad. So, what do we need? A hand that helps us handle the mistakes, or maybe the situation. First, **making mistakes is our nature, accepting mistakes is our culture** and **correcting them shows our stature**. Isn’t it? I do not know who said it, but I agree completely. Basically, I believe making, accepting and correcting mistakes is LIFE. What do you think?

> ***Simple Broken Rock Vs Statue.***

I want you to think about the stories of a few great personalities we have come across in our lives. I will give you a challenge: we remember them only because of their ability to fix the obstacles.

**Poverty** gives us WALT DISNEY, **Chronic Depression** gives us Abraham Lincoln, **Learning Difficulties** gives us Winston, **Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS)** gives us Stephen Hawking. **Polio and Paralysis attack** gives us Franklin D Roosevelt. **Hearing Inability** gives us Helen Keller, **Child Abuse** gives us Oprah Winfrey, and many more.

Life always challenges you, because it believes in you more than you believe in yourself. Challenges (not difficulties) always make us stronger and help us grow and develop. Soon a beautiful butterfly comes out of the cocoon. Remember, if we are not ready to accept the chiselling, we will always be a simple, useless rock rather than a beautiful statue.

> ***Balanced Approach vs Self Made.***

Have you ever noticed how much of our life depends on being centred and not bending towards either end? All highly successful people credit their achievements to their energy, ambition, environment, efforts and ventures. They believe it is their hustle alone that has brought them to where they are in society. In fact, I sometimes feel they are haughty in attitude and tone. After all the struggle and war, they have moved into a good position in society (one end). There are also people who credit their success to the right people or opportunities that came their way, maybe KARMA or LUCK. These people are very honest in acknowledging others for their success. I can say “They are BLESSED” (the other end). Success comes from BALANCE in the actions we perform. Directing our independence, will and choice in the right way brings SUCCESS. Those who believe they depend on others must balance their modesty with their self-esteem. Right? Unless you drive your car, Google Maps will never help you, right? We should believe everybody has an important role in someone’s journey of success. *Success is a combination of belief in our worthiness and the experience of our insignificance. A balanced approach will always give you success along with satisfaction.*

> ***Do not Hurry or Do not Worry !!!***

There lived a farmer who planted spinach and mango seeds and cared for them well. The farmer was delighted to see the spinach grow quickly out of the earth, bright green, after a few days. But he was very disappointed, as nothing came out of the mango seed. Still, he never gave up. After a year, the spinach had sprouted vibrant, luxuriant and green, and nothing had come out of the mango seed. One more year passed, and still he did not quit. One more year passed, and the farmer saw a tiny sprout emerge from the earth, so tiny compared to the spinach, but in the next six months the mango tree shot up to its height. What do you observe here? In those 3 to 4 years, the mango was growing its roots and giving itself all it needed to survive in the years to come. Isn’t it true that LIFE does not give you a challenge you cannot handle? Take a difficulty you are facing now, and know for sure that you have the capacity to handle it.

Remember, all through our time of struggle, you have been developing like a mango tree. Spinach and mango are different, but both make life beautiful and healthier by serving a different purpose.

Wait for your TIME, you will reach your destiny, Your Destiny, not someone else’s. Remember that India is almost 10 hours ahead of the USA, but that does not make India faster and the USA slower. So, relax and wait for your TIME: you are neither too early nor too late, just on TIME.

> ***Blaming the Situation.***

Luck or Destiny? Have I got the role of a successful person or not? Or do I need to work hard? So, in my ideology, the situations that come to you are beyond your control. Did we choose our parents, or our face, or our beauty? No, isn’t it? It is destiny. No one looks as good as their social media profile, and no one looks as ugly as their government ID card, right? So, your situations are destiny: you did not choose them, and they simply came to you. But my response to my situation is my CHOICE, and the response is not destiny. Situations remain what they are, but choice does not, and responses do not.

> ***What is your SEED.***

Have you ever felt nothing worked out after many attempts or a lot of hard work? Please remember: when GOD, or anything you believe in, says YES to you, it will give you what you want; if it says NO, it will give you something better; and if it says WAIT, it will give you the best. What if our land doesn’t suit wheat? Go for veggies, or go for flowers. The land may be fertile at some point, but the seed is not known. So, discover your SEED.

> ***Stopping the Interference.***

A great sportsman was asked how he managed his coaching skill at the age of 67. He replied, **“The ball has no idea how old I am. I concentrate only 20 to 30% on hitting the ball, and the rest is huge.”** Psychologically, the key factor in performing well in sports or in life is one and the same: the ability to control your quality and quantity. In simple words, controlling the dialogue you hear in your heart, by simply stopping the interference you hear inside, will make you a success.

> ***Limiting Vs Empowering Ourselves.***

A famous quote says,

*If Everybody can do it, I certainly can do it. If Nobody can do it, then I must do it.*

I also heard a quote that says,

*If Everybody can do it, let them do it. If Nobody can do it, then how can I do it?*

Just because you have joined a good college, school, job or company, you do not become an expert. The success of an organization is not your personal success. You must learn, work hard, cooperate, focus on the right process and craft your own success. Just because your horoscopes match, your relationship does not go deep. In anything you take on, you must either limit yourself or empower yourself.

> ***Soda or water … Why or how …***

There are always people who are out of control. Can we stop someone who is sledging you? I remember one of our family friends had a situation. I usually call him uncle. His daughter had a fight with her husband. After the fight she called her mother (Aunty) and said that she could not handle her husband and needed a change for at least 2 weeks. So, she decided to stay with her parents. But her mother replied, no dear, you should face the situation, you please stay there, and I will come to your place for 2 months! So funny!!!

Are our situations in our control most of the time? No, right? Just think about whether you want to be soda, which always foams up after being shaken, or stay calm like water when someone provokes you or complains about you. I know many will say soda was tested under pressure while water was not. I agree 100%, but what is complaining all about? It comprises circumstance, difficulty, conflict and fear, but response is something that combines your action and attitude, right? We always have a choice we are free to make, and we stand between complaining and responding.

***The Greatest personalities in the world were not born in a maternity ward. They were born in choices and responses.***

> ***Like, Share and Subscribe yourself first.***

This is one of the most inspiring and beautiful stories I have heard. There is a monastery where you are allowed to speak just two words after completing every 5 years, and for the rest of the time you must follow silence. One day, the Guru of that monastery asked a monk who had joined 5 years earlier to say his two words. He said, “HARD BED”. The Guru laughed sarcastically and asked him to maintain silence for the next 5 years. Again 5 years passed, and this time the monk said, “STINKY FOOD”. Again 5 years passed, and this time the monk said, “I QUIT”. Guys, please remember: in our life, to serve a higher purpose, we should face inconvenience voluntarily. Of course, the monk may have lived in a comfort zone before he joined the monastery, and it may have been hard for him to focus on what he was doing and stop complaining. Quitting is not always the right solution; just think of why you started doing it in the first place. **True Power or Potential always lies outside your comfort zone.**

> ***Journey of Life is important.***

No right things are always right, and no wrong things are always wrong. I remember, some years back, reading a story of 5 friends who experienced beautiful moments in their lives. On one of their treks, they got lost in a vast forest and were trying to find the way out. The first said that he was going to follow his intuition, and he decided to go left from where they were standing. The second decided to go right. The third decided to go back the same way they had come, as he believed that was the safest way. The fourth decided to walk forward, as he believed he was on the right track. But the fifth man said, I will climb up a tree to find the right path, or the shortest path. And they all started their journeys on the paths they had decided.

We may think the fifth man is right, since he rises above the situation and sees the larger picture. This decision sounds right to most of us. Yes, of course, but there is a twist in the story. Let’s understand them. Finding your own path gains you a different experience. It is not just about right or wrong. The first man reached a town after a while. The man who went right had to fight wild animals, but now he is experienced in forest treks. The man who went back joined the next group of trekkers, and what happened? He got a friend, right? The man who went straight found a farm and had a nice time, and the man in the tree found the shortest path.

The journey exposed each one of them in their own unique way. Life is an exam: you might get by if you copy others, but you cannot live with others’ experience. Your current chapter is not the same as someone else’s current chapter.

**You can learn from others, but not live with others’ experience.**

> ***What’s your value.***

Do you think you are not recognized for your work? Do you feel bored? Have you felt you are not valuable? If so, what is your value? How do you define it? Let’s see…

This is a real incident that happened to me. I found a valuable old camera while cleaning my home. I believed it had a certain value, and I wanted to check that value. So, I thought I would check the resale value of the camera. First, I took the camera to a camera resale and repair shop to check its value. After checking my camera, the shopkeeper said that he could give me $100 for it. Then I left and went to a pawn shop to check the collateral value of my camera. After seeing my camera, the pawn shop owner said he could not give anything for this scrap; it was not going to be useful for them. Then I went to an antique museum where all old things are showcased. I showed it to the manager of the museum, and he exclaimed, “This is priceless, what a quality of leather cover and German-made metals! I can give you a million dollars for this.” I was really gobsmacked by his words. But I still did not sell it. Do you know why? I do not want to stab the duck before it lays the golden egg.

Now I understand: you will be valued if you are in the right place. In the camera resale and repair shop, the reseller could see only the nuts and bolts in it. In the pawn shop, for collateral, they look at everything based on market value. But the museum, where old things are showcased, sees beyond the material: the emotions, the war behind the camera, the people who used the camera, right? If you feel that you are not valued, do not get frustrated, do not feel bored. Seek help from those who see your real value; they may be the senior people in your office.

There is no letter that cannot be turned into a book, no plant or tree that has no medicinal value, and no person in the world who has no value. Please remember this in your life.

So far, we have come across many stories, characters, analogies, places, situations, mistakes, morals, lessons, life, motivation, friendly advice, questions, queries and many more. But what do you infer from this? I believe all the aspects mentioned above have already been taught to you, right? You may already feel those things, right? Do you really understand the meaning of motivation? Motivation is something you believe comes from external objects or persons. Is that right? No, there is no word or term in the world called motivation. Of course, you may say it exists, but it always exists in the form of SELF. We have a word called SELF-MOTIVATION, meaning unless you are ready to move on, even God cannot help you.

In the contexts above, if you really noticed well, all the good changes, or even mistakes, happen only through your response. I may not have mentioned your stories above, or even my stories, but reality is a secret and common to all… Friends, this is the secret I am going to reveal to you.

> YOU are the SECRET, YOU are the CAUSE, YOU are the trigger, YOU are the TIME, YOU are your strength, YOU are your weakness, YOU are your OBSTACLES.

Be ready to listen to your heart. Unless YOU step in and listen, nothing can ever be changed. So
